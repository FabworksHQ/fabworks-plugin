# Materials and finishes

Fabworks stocks a deliberately limited catalog. The grade families below are documented; exact thicknesses, tube profiles, and per-thickness limits are live catalog data. Query `find_materials` on the Fabworks MCP server for current stock, or cite the fabworks.com materials pages. Never present a grade, thickness, or profile as stocked without checking.

## Sheet materials

| Grade | Documented facts |
| --- | --- |
| Aluminum 5052-H32 | General-purpose aluminum balancing strength, formability, and cost. Good for welding, machining, and bending. In the documented bending range. |
| Aluminum 6061-T6 | Heat-treatable structural alloy. Strong, corrosion resistant, weldable, good fatigue strength. Not in the documented bending material list. |
| Aluminum 7075-T6 | High-strength alloy; the docs describe 6061 as combining the robustness of 7075 with the malleability of 5052. Not in the documented bending material list. |
| Steel 1008 | Cold-rolled mild steel. Better edges, surface, and dimensional consistency than hot-rolled; suited to bending and fabrication. Most cost-effective material family. |
| Steel A36 | Hot-rolled pickled and oiled (HRPO) structural steel. Strong, workable, weldable, with a cleaner surface than plain hot-rolled. |
| Galvanized Steel G90 | Carbon steel hot-dip galvanized with 0.90 oz/sq ft zinc. Corrosion resistant with sacrificial zinc protection; suited to outdoor and damp environments. Galvanized coating can complicate welding. |
| Stainless Steel 304-2B | Corrosion and oxidation resistant, weldable, formable, easy to sanitize. Common for food service, marine, and high-exposure use. |

316 stainless is mentioned in Fabworks docs but has no materials page; verify availability through the catalog or support before recommending it.

## Tube materials

| Grade | Documented facts |
| --- | --- |
| Aluminum 6061-T6 | Lightweight, corrosion resistant, weldable, heat-treatable. Round and square profiles are documented; check the catalog for exact profiles. |
| Steel A513 | Welded mechanical tubing. Economical, formable, weldable; good for prototyping and production. |
| Steel A519 | Seamless carbon and alloy mechanical tubing. Costs more than A513; suited to high-pressure, torsional, and critical structural use where weld-seam failure is a concern. |

## Finishes

- The default finish is No Deburring. In quote requests, a missing finish means No Deburring.
- Deburring removes sharp edges. It is an edge treatment, not a cosmetic coating.
- Powder coating is the coated finish. The color list is live catalog data at fabworks.com/resources/guidelines/powder-coating; a color name alone can be ambiguous, so resolve it to an exact catalog finish.
- Raw material appearance is never a finish specification.

Powder coating rules:

- Adds 0.002" to 0.004" of thickness to each surface. Account for it on press fits and sub-flush countersinks.
- Every part needs a small hole or cutout for hanging. If a part has none, Fabworks reaches out to ask where to add one.
- Masking of holes or surfaces must be arranged with support before placing the order.
