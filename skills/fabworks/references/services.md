# Fabworks services and boundaries

Fabworks is an online laser cutting service. Upload a 3D STEP file, get an instant quote with DFM checks, and order. All parts are produced in the Fabworks facility in Ventura, CA, USA.

## Services

Core services:

- 2D laser cutting of flat sheet metal parts
- Sheet metal bending
- Tube laser cutting of round, square, and rectangular tube in steel and aluminum

Add-on operations on the same order:

- Tapping
- Countersinking
- Counterboring
- Hardware installation (PEM nuts, studs, standoffs, rivnuts)
- Powder coating

Welding and anodizing are documented as coming soon and available per request. Do not promise either; direct the user to support@fabworks.com.

## Boundaries

- Engraving is not offered, on sheet or tube.
- Only STEP or STP files are accepted. Parts are cut directly from the 3D model; flat patterns for bent parts are unfolded automatically. 2D formats such as DXF cannot be processed.
- A multipart STEP file is accepted and split into individual components, each quoted separately.
- Materials, thicknesses, and tube profiles outside the stocked catalog cannot be quoted online. Ask support about anything not in the catalog.
- Machined parts, castings, and other non-sheet, non-tube geometry are outside Fabworks services.
- Quoting and checkout happen on fabworks.com. The MCP server creates and configures quotes but does not submit checkout or payment.

## Size limits

| Service | Documented limits |
| --- | --- |
| Sheet | Max part 96" x 47". Min part 0.25" x 0.25". No minimum order quantity. Parts that will be bent are limited to 80 lbs. |
| Tube | Max finished length 96". No minimum length or order quantity. End-of-stock drop-off is priced automatically. |

## Lead times

Documented lead times, driven by part complexity and quantity:

- Small orders can be delivered as soon as next day when ordered before 9am PST on a weekday.
- Larger orders typically take 1 to 3 weeks.
- For faster than quoted, contact support@fabworks.com.

Do not promise a specific lead time for a specific quote; report what the quote or order status returns.
